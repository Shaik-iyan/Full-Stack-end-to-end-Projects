Frieren

Frieren: Beyond Journey's End follows an immortal elven mage who realizes the fleeting value of human life only after her former hero companion passes away from old age. To make amends, she embarks on a nostalgic new journey across the continent to better understand humanity and reconnect with his spirit.

Attack On Titan

Attack on Titan (Shingeki no Kyojin) follows humanity's desperate struggle for survival inside walled cities against giant, man-eating monsters known as Titans. The story transforms from a brutal survival horror into a complex political tragedy after a young soldier, Eren Yeager, vows to eradicate every Titan but uncovers dark secrets about the world's history and his own powers.

Demon Slayer

Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba follows Tanjiro Kamado, a kindhearted boy who joins the elite Demon Slayer Corps to find a cure for his sister, Nezuko, after she is turned into a demon and their family is slaughtered. Together, they fight alongside eccentric allies to defeat powerful, bloodthirsty demons and hunt down the ancient demon lord responsible for their tragedy.

Jujutsu Kaisen

ujutsu Kaisen follows high schooler Yuji Itadori, who swallows a rotting finger to save his friends, accidentally becoming the host to Sukuna, the legendary and malevolent "King of Curses." To stay alive and protect others, Yuji joins a secret society of Jujutsu Sorcerers to hunt down Sukuna's remaining fingers while fighting deadly, emotion-driven curses alongside powerful allies like Satoru Gojo.

That Time I Reincarnated as a Slime

That Time I Got Reincarnated as a Slime (often called Tensura) follows Rimuru Tempest, a regular 37-year-old corporate worker who dies in the real world and wakes up in a fantasy universe as a seemingly weak slime monster. Using his overpowered unique skills to absorb abilities and befriend diverse magical creatures, he accidentally builds a thriving, peaceful nation of monsters while navigating global politics and rival empires.

Horimiya

Horimiya follows the unexpected romance between Kyoko Hori, a popular, smart high school girl who is actually a domestic homebody, and Izumi Miyamura, a gloomy looking classmate who is secretly a heavily pierced and tattooed gentle soul. After accidentally discovering each other's hidden, out-of-school personas, they form a close bond that quickly blossoms into a heartwarming, realistic story about acceptance, friendship, and young love.
